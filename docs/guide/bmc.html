Sonae Zitoun · Business Model Canvas · Tunisian market

Margine to the groves, grignon to the fire, proof on the chain

A planning, traceability and marketplace platform for Tunisia's olive mills (maâsras), their neighbouring farmers, and the regulator. Launch region: Sfax olive belt, 2026–27 season.

8Key partners

Who we can't do it without

  • Regulators: ANPE (environment), the regional agriculture offices (CRDA) and the Ministry of Agriculture: they approve groves and accept the ledger as proof.
  • Office National de l'Huile (ONH) and the mill federations: access to mills and an endorsement for the Zero-Spill label.
  • Institut de l'Olivier (Sfax) and accredited labs: agronomic validation of doses, margine analyses.
  • UTAP / farmer GDAs: reach farmers and organise the receiving groves.
  • Tanker operators (private and mill-owned): drivers, GPS and flow meters as the delivery oracle.
  • Grignon buyers: pomace-oil extraction units, brickworks, hammams, bakeries, greenhouse heating.
  • Telecom: Tunisie Telecom / Ooredoo / Orange bulk SMS for farmers without smartphones.
  • Tech: Anthropic (AI), Vercel + Neon (hosting, database), Ethereum ecosystem (public ledger).
  • Funding: GIZ, EU and FAO environmental programmes, APIA and BFPME grants, Startup Act label.

7Key activities

What we must do every day

  • Daily spreading plan per mill: weather, caps, distances, fullest pond first.
  • Onboarding mills and mapping/approving receiving groves with the CRDA.
  • Running the grignon marketplace and its escrow.
  • Keeping the on-chain ledger and issuing Zero-Spill certificates.
  • AI support: lab reading, Ask Sonae (Arabic, French), season plans.
  • Field support during the harvest (Nov–Feb).

6Key resources

What we own

  • Planning engine encoding the spreading rules (tested, auditable).
  • ZitounLedger smart contract and the Zero-Spill certificate.
  • Grove registry: slope, soil, well and wadi distances, season caps.
  • Live platform: web, Android, PWA, Arabic and English.
  • Data: every season's loads, ponds and rain, improving the plans each year.
  • Small team: product, engineering, one agronomist, field agents.

2Value propositions

Why they choose Sonae

For the mill: never overflow, never get finedA day-by-day plan that tells each tanker where it may legally go, so the pond is emptied before the rain. Compliance proof is ready for any inspection.
For the farmer: free water and potashMargine brings water and about 4 kg of K₂O per m³, delivered to the field, under the legal cap, never before rain. It saves fertiliser money.
Grignon becomes incomeDried pomace sold as fuel (about 2.5 kg replaces 1 kg of diesel) to verified buyers, with payment held in escrow.
For the regulator: proof without paperworkEvery load is traced on a public ledger: nobody, us included, can edit it afterwards.
Zero-Spill certificateA QR code on the bottle proving the oil was made without polluting: an export and premium argument for Tunisian oil. New: this doesn't exist today.
Built for TunisiaArabic and French, SMS in Tunisian darija, live local weather, prices in TND.

4Customer relationships

How we keep them

  • Field onboarding at the mill before the season: pond, trucks, groves.
  • Self-service after that: the app, the AI assistant, and SMS for farmers.
  • A personal season plan generated for every account.
  • Harvest-time hotline (WhatsApp / phone).
  • End-of-season report: m³ moved, fines avoided, money saved.
  • Community: GDA meetings, the Zero-Spill label as a badge of pride.

3Channels

How we reach them

  • Direct sales to mills in Sfax, then Sousse, Mahdia, Monastir and Kairouan, before the November harvest.
  • Through the CRDA and ANPE (compliance campaigns) and mill federations.
  • UTAP and GDA meetings for farmers; word of mouth between neighbours.
  • Web app, Android app and PWA; bulk SMS.
  • Agricultural fairs (e.g. SIAMAP) and olive-oil events; Facebook groups for farmers.

What the mill gains

  • No overflow fine and no emergency pumping.
  • Grignon sold as fuel instead of piling up.
  • Inspection-ready proof, and the Zero-Spill label for its buyers.

Why now, and why us

  • The problem is seasonal and urgent: ponds fill in 3 months, rain storms hit the same weeks.
  • Rules exist, proof doesn't: controlled spreading is allowed but hard to check. The ledger gives the check.
  • Export pressure: European buyers ask for traceability and environmental proof.
  • No direct competitor combines daily planning, traceability and a grignon market for Tunisian mills.
  • Working product: live web and Android app, AI in Arabic, contract on a public testnet.

Main risks and answers

  • Mills slow to pay: start free for the pilot, price below one fine.
  • Regulator acceptance: pilot co-designed with the Sfax CRDA.
  • Seasonality: off-season revenue from certificates, data and regulator contracts.
