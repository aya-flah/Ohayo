2

No power, no water.

Summer 2026 was the hottest ever recorded in Tunisia. When the grid could not keep up, power cuts stopped the pumps, and farmers ran out of water for their fields.

In numbers

  • Peak demand in July 2026: about 6,400 MW (an estimate), up from 5,175 MW in 2025.
  • Peak hours run from 13:00 to 17:00, exactly when pumps run for daytime irrigation.
  • In Kairouan, pepper plots rotted when irrigation stopped (UTAP, July 2026).
  • A backup generator costs 50,000 to 100,000 TND. Most farmers can't afford one.

Who we help: the GDA. A GDA (Groupement de Développement Agricole) is a farmers' association that shares one well, one pump and one pipe network. Tunisia has about 2,500 of them. When the pump stops, every farmer in the GDA loses water at the same time.

Sources: Webdo, 8 Sep 2026; Tunisie Numérique, 16 Jul 2026; Business News, 24 Jul 2026; Webmanagercenter, 2020. Links are in the plan doc.

3

Our idea: the water tank is a battery.

Electricity is hard to store. Water is easy to store. So Sonae pumps while there is energy, and lets the water flow down later, when the power is gone.

1

Look ahead

Sonae reads tomorrow's weather and the power cuts STEG has announced.

2

Pump early

It fills the high tank while power exists: at midday with the sun, at night, or just before a cut.

3

Water by gravity

When the cut comes, water flows down from the tank to the fields. The pump is not needed.

4

Share fairly

Every farmer gets a turn by SMS, and the most fragile crops go first.

If you remember one sentence: Sonae makes sure the water is already up there before the power goes out, and that every farmer gets a fair turn.

Does it really work? A tank 10 m up gives about 1 bar of pressure. That is enough for furrows and low-pressure drip. If a GDA has no high tank, Sonae still moves pumping away from the hours when cuts happen.

4

A day with Sonae.

This is our demo story. Hédi grows tomatoes in a GDA near Kairouan. Learn it by heart: we tell it in the pitch.

Monday 14:00

The forecast says 46 °C tomorrow. STEG announces a cut from 10:00 to 16:00.

Monday 14:05

Sonae makes a new plan: pump tonight and from 06:00 to 10:00, so the tank is full at 10:00.

Monday 20:00

Every farmer gets an SMS. Hédi's says:

Dourek ghodwa 5h–7h, parcelle 3

Your turn tomorrow, 5 to 7 am, plot 3.

Tuesday 05:00

Hédi's tomatoes are flowering, their most fragile stage, so they are watered first, in the cool morning.

Tuesday 10:00

The power cut starts. The pump stops. The tank is full.

10:00 to 16:00

The other farmers' turns run by gravity from the tank.

Tuesday 16:00

Power returns. Sonae refills the tank and records every litre delivered.

The result: no crop lost, no diesel burned, and no argument about whose turn it was.

5

The main feature: the 48-hour plan.

Everything else in Sonae exists to make this plan better. If a feature does not help the plan, we don't build it.

What goes in

  • Weather: heat, sun and rain, from Open-Meteo
  • STEG cuts: typed in, or read by the AI from a screenshot
  • Crops: who grows what, on how much land, how thirsty
  • Equipment: tank size, pump power, solar panels
Sonae engine

The same code runs on the server, on the phone and on the Node.

What comes out

  • When to pump, and with which energy
  • The tank level, hour by hour
  • Turns: who irrigates when
  • One SMS for each farmer
  • Alerts when water will be short

How it decides

1

Work out how much water each plot needs. A hotter day means more water.

2

Put the turns in the cool hours: early morning and evening.

3

Make sure the tank is full before each turn and before each cut.

4

Use the cheapest energy first: sun, then the grid, and diesel only if nothing else works.

5

Give a reason for every decision, like "Pumping 06:00 to 10:00 because of the cut at 10:00".

No hardware? It still works. The manager types the tank level into the app, and farmers get their SMS. The Node only makes it automatic and measured.

6

Four extensions, each one helps the plan.

The plan is the heart. These four make it run by itself, make it trusted, let neighbours share, and make it easy to talk to.

Sonae Node and 3D twin

Makes the plan run by itself.

A small box (an ESP32) on the pump and tank measures the water level, the flow and the solar power, and runs the plan even when offline. A 3D model of the farm in the app moves with its data.

Water Ledger

Makes the plan trusted.

Every delivered turn is recorded in litres, in a log nobody can secretly change. Its daily fingerprint is stored on the blockchain. Fair bills, fewer disputes.

Yui exchange

Lets neighbours share.

Farmers share or sell spare water and solar energy, paid through a smart contract on the Sepolia test network. No middleman. See the next page.

Ask Sonae (Claude AI)

Makes the plan easy to talk to.

Reads STEG notices from a screenshot, answers "what if" questions in Derja or French, and writes the SMS.

Not this weekend: crop photo diagnosis, voice calls, an agrivoltaics calculator. The culture game is dropped.

7

Yui: neighbours sharing water and energy.

Yui (結) is the Japanese village tradition of neighbours helping each other with farm work. In Sonae, a farmer with spare water or solar energy can share or sell it to a neighbour, with no middleman taking a cut.

1. List

Ali has 5,000 litres to spare this week. He posts an offer with a price.

2. Pay

Sami pays with MetaMask. The money is locked in the smart contract, not sent to Ali yet.

3. Deliver

The water flows. The Node's flow meter measures it, or Sami confirms it arrived.

4. Release

The contract pays Ali. If nothing arrives in time, Sami gets his money back.

Why a blockchain here?

  • Nobody in the middle takes a cut.
  • Nobody can change the record afterwards.
  • The rules (lock, release, refund) are code, not promises.

Try it yourself (website)

  1. Install the MetaMask extension in Chrome and create a test account.
  2. Switch MetaMask to the Sepolia test network.
  3. Get free test ETH from a Sepolia faucet.
  4. Open the Exchange page in Sonae and press "Connect wallet".
  5. Post an offer with one account, buy it with a second, then confirm delivery.

Test ETH has no value. Never use a real wallet for the demo, and never share a private key, not even with the team.

Selling electricity between private people needs approval in Tunisia, so we show it on a test network.

How it's built.

One plan, kept in three places: the server, the app and the Node. If one is cut off, the others keep going.

One repo for everything

apps/webThe website (React). It installs like an app and works offline.
apps/mobileThe same website wrapped as an Android app with Capacitor. One code, two apps.
apps/apiThe server (Node.js): plans, SMS, AI, ledger, the delivery oracle.
packages/engineThe planner. Everyone uses this same package.
firmwareThe ESP32 code. It runs in Wokwi and on the real board.
contractsThe smart contracts for Yui and the ledger, on Sepolia.

9

Works offline. Stays safe.

A power cut often means no internet too. Sonae is built for that day.

Without internet

  • Farmers only need SMS, and SMS works without internet.
  • The app keeps the plan on the phone and can make a new one offline.
  • The manager's phone can send the SMS itself.
  • The Node runs the plan from its memory, with its own clock.
  • Everything syncs when the internet comes back.
  • Needs internet: fresh weather, the AI, the blockchain.

Pitch trick: switch on airplane mode during the demo. The app keeps working.

Security

  • Each Node signs every message with a secret key, so fake data is rejected.
  • Only managers can control pumps, and every command is logged.
  • Safety rules live on the Node: stop the pump when the tank is full or the well runs dry.
  • The Claude key stays on the server, never in the app. Spending stops at $20.
  • The AI cannot switch pumps or send SMS by itself.
  • The smart contracts are tested, refund after a deadline and have an emergency pause.
  • We keep only a farmer's name, phone and plot (Tunisian law 2004-63).

AI proposes, the engine checks, a person approves, the device enforces.

The hardware, real or simulated.

Tonight we build the Node in Wokwi, a free online ESP32 simulator. If we get the parts, we flash the same code onto the real board.

PartIts job
ESP32The brain: reads the sensors and runs the plan
HC-SR04Measures how full the tank is
YF-S401Counts the litres that flow to the plots
INA219 + 6 V solar panelMeasures the solar power available
DS3231Keeps the time when there is no internet
12 V pump + relayLifts water to the tank, switched on and off by the ESP32
Servo + small valveOpens the gravity line to the plots
LEDs + button + power bankShow the grid and pump state; the button fakes a cut; the power bank keeps the Node alive

The magic moment: unplug the 12 V adapter. That is a real power cut. The pump stops, the Node stays on, sees the cut and opens the valve, and the water keeps flowing by gravity. In the app, the 3D farm shows the same thing live.

Words we use.

Bansui (番水)
The Japanese practice of taking turns for irrigation water. Our turn scheduler copies it.
CRDA
The regional agriculture office (Commissariat Régional au Développement Agricole).
Derja
Tunisian Arabic. Our SMS use Latin letters: 160 characters fit in one SMS, against 70 in Arabic script.
ESP32
A small, cheap computer board with Wi-Fi. The brain of the Node.
Escrow
Money locked by the smart contract until the delivery is confirmed.
ET0
How much water plants lose to the air in a day. Hot, sunny and windy days mean more.
GDA
A farmers' association that runs a shared well, pump and pipe network.
Gravity irrigation
Water flowing down from a high tank, with no pump.
Kc
A crop's thirst factor at its current growth stage.
Ledger
A list of records that nobody can secretly change.
MetaMask
A browser wallet. It signs blockchain payments.
MQTT
A light way for devices to send messages to a server.
Oracle
The trusted meter (our Node and server) that tells the contract what was delivered.
PWA
A website that installs like an app and works offline.
Sepolia
Ethereum's free test network. Its test ETH has no value.
Smart contract
A program on the blockchain that holds the rules and the money.
Sonae (備え)
"Being prepared". Our name.
STEG
Tunisia's national electricity and gas company.
UTAP
The Tunisian union of farmers and fishers.
Wokwi
A free online simulator for ESP32 and Arduino boards.
Yui (結)
The Japanese village tradition of neighbours helping each other. Our sharing feature.
