Kizuna 2026 · Demo script · For whoever drives the laptop

Sonae Zitoun: click this, say that

Every feature in the order we show it to the jury: what to click, what you should see, and what to say. Use it once alone as a test run, then use it live.

Live demo: about 7 minutes for the full tour, about 4 minutes for the short version (parts marked core). Backup: the 3-minute video in docs/video/Sonae_Zitoun_Demo.mp4.

0. Before the jury arrives (10 minutes)

  1. In one terminal, at the repo root, run npm run dev:api. Wait for the line saying it listens on port 8787.
  2. In a second terminal, run npm run dev:web, then open http://localhost:5173 in Chrome, full screen.
  3. Open apps/api/.env and keep it at hand: it holds the demo logins (admin, mill owner, farmer). Never show this file on the projector.
  4. MetaMask: select the Sepolia test network and the demo wallet (0x60Ea…F0AD). It is the regulator, the mill and the farmer of the 6 demo groves. Make sure it has some test ETH (it had about 0.07).
  5. Optional, for the grignon purchase: a second MetaMask account with about 0.01 test ETH (send it from the demo wallet). A mill can't buy its own grignon.
  6. Click the moon/sun button at the top right if the room is very bright (Field mode reads better on projectors). Check that the language button shows ع (you are in English).
  7. Sign out of any account (Account → Sign out) so the demo starts clean. On the Plan page, choose Storm demo.
If the internet is weak: the Plan, Twin, Process and Replay pages work offline. The AI features, the live forecast and the blockchain need internet: switch to the video for those parts.

1. Opening core · 20 s

Say: "Every winter, Tunisia's olive mills, the maâsras, press their olives and are left with two wastes: margine, a dark water that pollutes wadis and wells when it's dumped, and fitoura, the pomace. Sonae plans what happens to both, every day: margine goes to olive groves as free water and potassium, fitoura goes to hammams, bakeries and kilns as fuel, and every load is recorded on a public ledger."

2. The core service: the 7-day plan core · 1 min 30 real engine

  1. Stay on the Plan page. Point at the big headline.
  2. Click the round green play button on the right of "The week, one decision a day". The days play; the 3D region behind the page follows, tankers drive.
  3. Click the play button again to pause, then click the "Tomorrow" card.
  4. Click one tanker trip in the list (a grove owner's name). Its reason opens: pond level, distance, cap left.
  5. Scroll down to The SMS. Click "Driver", then "Farmer".
  6. Scroll back up. Click "Add rain", choose Thursday, set about 20 mm, click "Add rain". Thursday becomes 44 mm and is paused; the headline still says no pond spills.
  7. Click the "+20 mm thursday" chip to remove it again.
Say: "This is the core: one decision a day. Three mills are pressing and a storm is coming on Wednesday. Sonae sends 640 m³ out on the two dry days, pauses during the rain and while the soil is wet, and no pond spills. Doing nothing would pour 114 m³ into the wadis this week.
Every trip has a reason: the fullest pond goes first, to the nearest grove that is allowed: never near a drinking well, never near the wadi, never on a slope, never above 50 m³ per hectare. Farmers and drivers get an SMS in Derja, on any phone. If the forecast changes, the plan changes."

Optional: My mill real forecast

  1. Click "My mill" at the top right of the Plan page, then "Edit your mill". Change the town or the pond size, click "Save and plan".
Say: "A mill can enter its own numbers: the plan now uses its pond, its tankers, its groves and the real forecast for its town, starting today."

3. The 3D twin 40 s

  1. Click "Twin" in the top menu. Drag the 3D scene to turn it.
  2. Click the green play button at the bottom. Tankers drive, ponds go down, groves darken; the dispatch log fills.
  3. Click "Make it rain". Clouds, the wadi lights up, tankers stop. Click "Stop the rain".
Say: "This is the whole region in 3D: three mills with their margine ponds, the olive groves, the drinking well with its safety ring, the wadi, and the buyers of fitoura. The groves with a red border can never receive margine. When it rains, everything stops: that's the rule that protects the wadis."

4. How the waste is transformed 40 s

  1. Click "Process" in the menu. Click the steps at the bottom one by one: Spread, Soil, Dry, Gas, Engine (or press play).
Say: "Two paths. Margine is spread as a thin film between the rows; in the soil, microbes break down the bitter part and the potassium reaches the roots. It never goes into water. Fitoura is dried in the sun, pressed into pellets, and a gasifier turns it into gas that can run the well's pump engine with 60 to 70% less diesel. The gasifier is a later step; today fitoura is sold as fuel."

5. Farmer tools: real data and AI core · 1 min 30

5a. Check my grove real forecast

  1. Click "Check" in the menu. Keep My grove selected.
  2. Pick a town (e.g. Agareb) or click "Use my location". Type an area, e.g. 12 ha.
  3. Click "Check my grove". Allowed or never, the season cap, and 7 days from the real forecast with the m³ allowed each day.
  4. Try a "no": set To nearest well to 60 m. "Can never receive margine." Put it back.
Say: "This isn't a simulation: a farmer enters his own grove and gets the real forecast for his exact place, with the days he can receive margine and how much."

5b. Lab report reader AI · Claude Opus

  1. Click "Lab report" at the top right.
  2. Click "Try a sample report" (or "Add a photo or PDF" and take a photo of a real report).
  3. Click "Read the report". About 6 seconds: the values (pH, potassium, polyphenols, salinity), then "Sonae's advice": up to 33 m³/ha, with reasons.
  4. Click "Use this reading on the passport". It opens Yui with the reading ready for the next load (used in part 7).
Say: "Mills get lab reports as paper or PDF, in French or Arabic. Our lab reader AI reads them, converts the units itself, and Sonae turns the values into a safe dose per hectare. Here the margine is rich in potassium, so Sonae lowers the dose to 33 m³ per hectare."

5c. Ask Sonae AI · Claude Opus

  1. Go back to Check and click "Ask Sonae".
  2. Click the suggestion "When can I spread margine near Agareb this week, for a 10 ha grove?" Or type a question in Derja: "Chnoua n3amel bel margine ki tsob chta ghodwa?"
  3. About 10 seconds. The answer shows "used: grove check" or "used: forecast": it called the planner and the real weather.
Say: "Ask Sonae answers in Derja, Arabic, French or English. It doesn't guess: it calls our planner and the real forecast, then explains the result simply."
If the jury asks what the AI is: "Anthropic's Claude Opus model through its API, behind our server, with a hard spending cap. The numbers always come from our engine; the AI reads documents, explains and writes plans around those numbers."

6. Accounts, packs and the AI season plan core · 2 min

6a. Packs

  1. Click "Sign in" at the top right, then the link "See the packs and prices".
  2. Switch Mill owner / Farmer. Move the slider: the prices update.
Say: "The mill pays most, because the waste is its problem: about half a dinar per m³, 950 dinars a season on Pro for most mills. Farmers can use Sonae for free, or pay 35 dinars a hectare to have their dose planned."

6b. Mill owner: season plan AI · Claude Opus

  1. Click "Sign in". Type the mill email and password from apps/api/.env. Click "Sign in".
  2. You land on the mill owner's space: the mill, the pack (Pro), the season balance.
  3. Click "Generate my season plan". About 25 seconds: the steps tick one by one. Keep talking (text below).
  4. When the plan appears, scroll slowly: headline and figures, costs and income bars, click a phase on the timeline, open a risk, tick two checklist items, last season's weather.
  5. Click the account button (initials, top right) → "Sign out".
Say (while it generates): "One button gives the mill owner his whole season. First our engine computes the real numbers from his mill and last season's weather: tanker trips, hectares needed, safe days, every cost and every dinar of fitoura sales. Then Sonae AI writes the plan around those numbers: phases from November to February, risks, a checklist. It's not allowed to change a number.
When it appears: "1,800 m³ of margine, 36 hectares of groves, 102 safe days, and a positive balance thanks to the fitoura. Each phase says what to do and what weather to expect."

6c. Farmer: plan for his land AI · Claude Opus

  1. Sign in with the farmer login from .env. If the farm form shows, fill Farm name and 12 ha, click "Save my farm".
  2. Click "Generate my season plan". Show the margine for his land, the loads, and the fertiliser saved. Sign out.
Say: "The farmer gets the same thing for his land: how much margine his trees really need, how many tanker loads, and how much he saves on potassium fertiliser."

6d. Admin

  1. Sign in with the admin login. Show the tiles, the packs and the users. Click "mills" and "farmers" to filter. Sign out.
Say: "The admin sees everything: users by role, who has which pack, the season revenue, the margine and hectares under plan, and how much the AI has cost. He can suspend an account. Passwords are hashed, sessions expire, and every action checks the role."

7. Blockchain: Yui, the margine passport core · 1 min 30 Sepolia + MetaMask

Say first: "A mill is legally responsible for its margine, but nobody can follow every tanker. Yui puts every load on a public record that no mill, farmer or official can quietly change. It runs on Sepolia, Ethereum's test network, so there's no real money."
  1. Click "Yui" in the menu. Without any wallet, it already reads the live contract: 6 groves approved by the regulator, loads, grignon lots, certificates.
  2. Point at the 4 steps: Dispatch → Confirm → Certify → Verify.
  3. Click "Connect MetaMask" and approve in MetaMask. Badges show: Regulator, Mill, Tanker meter, Farmer.
  4. Dispatch. In "Dispatch a tanker load": choose grove #1 Hedi Ben Ali, 15 m³ (the lab reading from part 5b is already filled in). Click "Dispatch 15 m³", confirm in MetaMask. After about 15 seconds the load appears "On the road"; the grove bar shows 15 reserved.
  5. Over the cap? Type 700 m³: the button says "Over the grove's cap". Put 15 back.
  6. Confirm. On the new load, click "Confirm", approve in MetaMask. "Delivered"; the grove now shows 15 m³ applied.
  7. Certify. In "Clean-oil certificates", keep batch EVOO-2026-017, 5000 L. Click "Certify this batch", approve.
  8. Verify. Type the certificate number (e.g. 1) in "Verify a certificate number". "Certificate #1 is genuine", with the mill's traced m³.
  9. Optional, grignon escrow: switch MetaMask to the second account, click "Buy with escrow" on the lot, 5 t, "Pay into escrow". Then "Delivered" on the order: the mill is paid.
  10. Optional: click the "ZitounLedger 0x094B…" chip to show the contract on Etherscan, and "Source verified on Sourcify".
Say while you click: "The mill records each tanker load: which grove, how many m³, and a fingerprint of the lab report. It pays Sonae's fee, which stays locked until delivery. The contract itself refuses any load beyond the grove's legal cap. The farmer, or the tanker's GPS meter, confirms the delivery. If nobody confirms within 48 hours, the load expires, the fee goes back and the mill gets a red flag.
Only a mill with every load traced can certify a batch of oil. That's our Zero-Spill certificate: a QR code on the bottle that any buyer, here or in Europe, can verify with no account and no middleman. Other traceability shows where the olives came from; Sonae shows the mill didn't pollute."
If MetaMask fails or the network is slow: stay on the read-only view (groves, loads, certificates are live) and say "every action is a real transaction; here are the ones we recorded". Each transaction takes about 15 seconds on Sepolia: keep talking while it confirms.

8. Proof on real weather 30 s

  1. Click "Replay". Point at the red line (without Sonae) and the brown line (with Sonae).
  2. Click "Wet winter (rain ×3)".
Say: "This is last winter in Sfax, real weather, day by day. Without Sonae, the ponds fill and spill 7,522 m³ of margine into the wadis on 78 days. With Sonae: none, and 11,800 m³ go back to olive groves as fertiliser. Even with three times the rain, the plan holds."

9. Arabic 20 s

  1. Click the ع button next to the moon. The whole site switches to Arabic, right to left. Open two pages (e.g. Check, Process). Click EN to go back.
Say: "Everything is available in Arabic, right to left, including the AI answers and the season plans. The SMS stay in Derja."

10. Closing core · 20 s

Say: "Sonae turns olive waste from a pollution problem into free fertiliser for farmers, cheap fuel for hammams and kilns, and proof of clean oil for exporters. Planned every day, enforced by the contract, verifiable by anyone. 備え, sonae: prepared, nothing to fear."

If something goes wrong

"Can't reach the Sonae server"
The API isn't running: run npm run dev:api. Until then, skip the AI and account parts.
"The AI budget is used up"
The 20 USD cap was reached: raise AI_BUDGET_USD in apps/api/.env and restart the API.
MetaMask on the wrong network
Click "Switch to Sepolia" on the Yui page, or pick Sepolia in MetaMask.
Anything else, or no time
Play the 3-minute video, then take questions with the jury Q&A in the team guide.
